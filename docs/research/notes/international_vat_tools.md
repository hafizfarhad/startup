# International VAT/GST on digital services: existing tools, free tables, datasets and comparisons (as of 2026-10-07)

Scope note: this covers the cross-border VAT/GST-on-digital-services side only (not US sales-tax nexus). "Non-resident zero threshold" below means a foreign seller must register from the first B2C sale. All prices are list prices as shown on the dated page; many vendors quote in USD or EUR with the other via a currency toggle.

## Key Question 1: Which tools automatically determine where a digital seller must register (thresholds by country, EU OSS €10k rule, non-resident zero thresholds)? Free tiers? Prices?

### Takeaway
Automatic "where must I register" monitoring for VAT/GST exists in at least four self-serve products (Quaderno, Stripe Tax, Lovat, Commenda) plus Kintsugi, but every one of them requires connecting live transaction data (or using the vendor as payment processor) and none offers a free, data-only wizard; the no-registration alternative is a merchant of record (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Dodo) at roughly 4-5% + 40-50c per transaction. Enterprise engines (Avalara, Vertex, Sovos, Fonoa, Taxually) are quote-only.

### Cited Findings

Self-serve tools with threshold/registration monitoring:
- Quaderno plans (monthly list): Hobby $24/mo (25 transactions, 1 jurisdiction, 1 user, 1 integration), Startup $41/mo (250 transactions), Business $83/mo (1,000), Growth $124/mo (2,500), Enterprise custom; "Nexus & threshold alerts" listed as a cross-plan feature ("If your business needs to register in a new place, you're notified right away"); 7-day free trial, no credit card — [Quaderno pricing](https://quaderno.io/pricing/)
- Quaderno Monitor claims threshold monitoring across "150+ countries", including the EU OSS "€10,000 per calendar year" threshold for EU-based sellers, and states "Businesses based outside the EU have no threshold and must collect EU VAT from the first sale" and "you must register for UK VAT before your first sale"; it works from "sales from every payment gateway and shopping cart" (requires connected integrations); data sources are not disclosed beyond "Local rules and threshold updates are maintained automatically" — [Quaderno Monitor](https://quaderno.io/product/monitor/)
- Third-party listings quote Quaderno "from $29/mo" (MakerStack) and "$49/month" starting price (GoodVat) — figures differ from the vendor page above — [MakerStack review](https://makerstack.co/reviews/quaderno-review/); [GoodVat comparison](https://goodvat.com/guides/cross-border-tax-tools/)
- Stripe Tax price: 0.5% per transaction where you are registered to collect; monitoring described as "Threshold monitoring based on volume and transactions, as well as VAT OSS threshold" with email and Dashboard alerts — [Stripe Tax](https://stripe.com/tax)

- Kintsugi: "$75 per filing or registration, in any country"; "tracks your sales in each country against that country's threshold" (vendor-authored list, dated March 27, 2026) — [Kintsugi: 9 Best Cross-Border VAT Compliance Software](https://trykintsugi.com/blog/best-cross-border-vat-compliance-software)

### Inferences
- The "where must I register" function is bundled, not sold alone: every monitoring product is a wrapper around the vendor's transaction feed (Stripe only sees Stripe payments; Quaderno/Lovat need connected gateways). A pre-sale, zero-data wizard ("I am in Pakistan, selling SaaS B2C to DE/UK/AU, ~€2k/month: where must I register and when?") is not what any of these offers for free.
- Price floor for self-serve monitoring is ~$24-35/month (Quaderno Hobby, Lovat Start-up), which only makes sense once a seller already has revenue; the MoR route costs ~2 percentage points over Stripe's base processing.
- Stripe's monitoring has known blind spots a neutral dataset can call out: no alerts under $10k prior-year revenue, home-country excluded, all sales assumed taxable at destination, and a 104-location list that omits Brazil, Malawi, Botswana and Pakistan.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether Quaderno's Hobby plan (1 jurisdiction) actually includes multi-country threshold alerts; the pricing page lists alerts as a general feature only.
- Taxually and Fonoa publish no prices anywhere I could reach; Fonoa's per-call price is unknown.
- Lovat's vendor price page (€35/€65/€450, annual billing) conflicts with third-party listings (€27/€50/€344, plus a free plan); the monthly-billing price was not displayed to the fetch.

## Key Question 2: Which free public tables exist of digital-services VAT/GST thresholds and rates by country? Maintainers, sourcing, dating, country counts, JSON/API?

### Takeaway
There are at least seven free vendor-maintained country tables (Quaderno 80, Avalara ~85, Fonoa 110+, Anrok 110+, Dodo 30+, Lovat 12 European, vatabout/vatupdate) plus the EY guide (153 jurisdictions, PDF) and PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries (150+ countries, prose); none of the vendor tables cites a tax-authority source per row, only two (Anrok, Dodo) separate resident from non-resident thresholds, and none offers JSON or an API.

### Cited Findings
- Quaderno "Digital taxes around the world": 80 countries/jurisdictions; "Created on May 8, 2023" / "Updated on Apr 15, 2026"; author credited as "Annie"; selective citations (ATO, Revenu Québec, Nepal tax authority) but not every entry; no JSON/API/download. Sample: Albania 20%, no threshold; Australia 10% GST, A$75,000; Japan 10%, JPY 10 million; Singapore 9% GST, dual threshold S$1m global and S$100k Singapore sales; South Africa 15%, ZAR 1,000,000; India 18% GST, no threshold — [Quaderno blog](https://quaderno.io/blog/digital-taxes-around-world-know-new-tax-rules/)
- Fonoa "Global Guide to 110+ Countries": published August 26, 2024, last updated March 4, 2026; the table has only country, standard rate, tax type and local name (no threshold column); the text says "non-EU businesses have an obligation to register for VAT in the EU as of their first sales, as zero threshold applies to them" and that the €10,000 rule is for the "EU-based seller"; no row-level tax-authority citations; no download/API; Philippines shown at 12%; Botswana mentioned only as "planning to introduce similar obligations"; Brazil and Malawi absent — [Fonoa guide](https://www.fonoa.com/resources/blog/global-vat-and-gst-on-digital-services)
- Avalara VATlive "Global VAT and GST on digital services": approximately 85 countries; no last-updated date; no per-row sources; no implementation dates. Sample: Australia 10%/AUD 75,000; Canada 5%/CAD 30,000; EU "€10,000 EU-wide cross-border threshold for EU-established microbusinesses; otherwise member state rules" (does not address non-EU sellers); India 18%/INR 2 million; Japan 10%/JPY 10 million; Mexico 16%/none; Singapore 9%/SGD 1 million — [Avalara VATlive table](https://www.avalara.com/vatlive/en/global-vat-gst-on-e-services.html)
- Anrok "VAT index for digital products and services": 110+ jurisdictions (including Canadian provinces); columns: tax rate, registration threshold for nonresidents (e.g., "First B2C sale"), and whether digital products are taxed; no B2B reverse-charge or effective-date columns; schema "dateModified" August 10, 2026; no sources cited; public, no paywall; CTA "Talk to our team" — [Anrok VAT index](https://www.anrok.com/vat-software-digital-services)
- Dodo Payments "VAT and GST Registration Thresholds by Country for Digital Sellers": 30+ countries; published September 20, 2026; columns: Threshold (Resident), Threshold (Non-Resident), Notes; no rates in the main table; general pointers to HMRC, BZSt, CRA, ATO, IRD, IRAS but no per-row citations; Philippines PHP 3 million present; Brazil present with 2026 reform note; Malawi and Botswana absent; no download — [Dodo thresholds](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/vat-gst-registration-thresholds); companion "Country-by-Country Cheat Sheet" — [Dodo cheat sheet](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/vat-compliance-digital-products)
- Lovat "VAT Registration Thresholds by Country 2026": 12 European markets (DE, FR, IT, ES, NL, PL, SE, AT, BE, IE, UK, NO); published June 25, 2026; non-eurozone figures in local currency with approximate euro equivalents at Q4 2025 FX; no tax-authority links; non-resident €0 noted; sample: Germany €0 for non-residents, Italy €85,000 residents/€0 non-residents, Poland PLN 200,000, Sweden SEK 120,000, Austria €42,000 — [Lovat blog](https://vatcompliance.co/blog/vat-registration-thresholds-by-country-2026/)
- VATupdate "VAT on Digital Services by Country: Rates, Thresholds, and Registration Rules": published October 1, 2026; "(partially) written with the help of AI"; original source cited as vatabout.com; FX "as of September 29, 2026"; sample non-resident thresholds: Andorra EUR 40,000; Bosnia BAM 100,000; Iceland ISK 2 million; Norway NOK 50,000; Ukraine UAH 1 million; Switzerland CHF 100,000; UK £90,000 residents/zero non-resident B2C digital; no mention of Philippines, Brazil, Malawi, Botswana changes — [VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/10/01/vat-on-digital-services-by-country-rates-thresholds-and-registration-rules/)
- VATabout describes itself as "an independent publication covering VAT, GST, and indirect tax compliance worldwide" with country guides for 120+ jurisdictions "written by working tax professionals"; ownership not disclosed on its About page — [VATabout About](https://vatabout.com/about); [VATabout resources](https://vatabout.com/resources)
- vatcalc.com (Richard Asquith, ex-KPMG/EY, ex-Avalara VATlive) maintains a "Global VAT & GST on digital services B2C & B2B" tracker and per-country pages (e.g., Brazil Aug 2026, Malawi Apr 2026, Botswana Jun 2026, Nigeria 2026, South Africa Apr 2025); the global page returned HTTP 403 and a bot-check to automated fetches, so its country count and update date could not be read — [vatcalc global tracker](https://www.vatcalc.com/global/global-vat-and-gst-on-digital-services-to-consumers/); [vatcalc B2B digital services](https://www.vatcalc.com/global/vat-on-cross-border-b2b-digital-services/)
- EY Worldwide VAT, GST and Sales Tax Guide 2026: 153 jurisdictions, content current as of 1 January 2026; chapters cover "VAT or GST provisions related to the digital economy (including online platforms)"; new chapters Cabo Verde, Cameroon, Ethiopia; revised Bhutan, Brazil, China; free PDF — [EY guide page](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-guides/worldwide-vat-gst-and-sales-tax-guide); [EY PDF](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/technical/tax-guides/documents/ey-gl-vat-guide-03-2026.pdf); [EY Tax News](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0799-worldwide-vat-gst-and-sales-tax-guide-2026)
- PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries: free, 150+ countries, written by local PwC specialists and updated through the year; digital-services thresholds appear in prose per country (e.g., Ukraine: non-residents supplying B2C digital services must register if VATable operations exceed UAH 1 million in the previous calendar year) — [PwC WWTS Ukraine](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/other-taxes); [PwC WWTS overview](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/services/tax/worldwide-tax-summaries.html)
- Tax Foundation "VAT Registration Thresholds in Europe" (June 15, 2026): 32 European countries; cites VATCalc, PwC WWTS, national tax authorities and an IMF paper; domestic thresholds only, no digital/non-resident distinction; "Spain and Turkey are the only countries that do not have a threshold" — [Tax Foundation](https://taxfoundation.org/data/all/eu/vat-registration-exemption-thresholds-europe/); its underlying data repo (reduced VAT rates, mostly from OECD Consumption Tax Trends) — [GitHub TaxFoundation/consumption-taxes](https://github.com/TaxFoundation/consumption-taxes)
- EU Commission OSS portal (official): Union and non-Union schemes; non-Union scheme for businesses without an EU establishment ("Being identified or required to be identified for VAT purposes in the EU does not prevent the taxable person from using the non-Union scheme"); the €10,000 threshold is a combined figure for cross-border TBE services plus intra-Community distance sales and requires the supplier be established in only one Member State; quarterly returns for Union/non-Union, monthly for import scheme; page carries no date — [EU OSS portal](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop_en)
- OECD Tax Policy Reforms 2026 (covering 2025 reforms across 92 jurisdictions): "more than 116 countries have now implemented reforms based on the OECD's international VAT standards for digital trade, with another 22 implementing or considering them"; 43 countries collect VAT on low-value imported goods; 27 jurisdictions changed VAT rates in 2025 — [OECD press release, Sept 2026](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/news/press-releases/2026/09/governments-reforming-tax-systems-to-boost-growth-but-revenue-pressures-continue-to-mount.html); [OECD report PDF](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2026/09/tax-policy-reforms-2026_96b6be0a/43d18a55-en.pdf); secondary summaries at [Innovate Tax](https://innovatetax.com/blog/oecd-tax-policy-2026/) and [VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/09/21/oecd-report-highlights-global-shift-to-digital-vat-enforcement/)
- Other free SEO tables found (vendor or affiliate): Vertex "Digital tax rules in operation across the globe" — [Vertex](https://www.vertexinc.com/resources/resource-library/digital-tax-rules-operation-across-globe); 1D3 guide — [1D3](https://www.1d3.com/blog/vat-compliance-for-digital-services); countrytaxcalc "VAT Rates by Country 2026" — [countrytaxcalc](https://www.countrytaxcalc.com/tax-guides/vat-rates-by-country-2026/)

### Inferences
- The tables are marketing assets: all are hosted by vendors (Quaderno, Fonoa, Avalara, Anrok, Dodo, Lovat, Vertex) or news aggregators; the only sourced tables (Tax Foundation, EY, PwC) do not isolate the non-resident digital-services threshold in a machine-readable form.
- The field most often missing or conflated is exactly the one a "where must I register" wizard needs: the non-resident threshold (as opposed to the domestic registration threshold) plus the effective date and the B2B reverse-charge/withholding treatment. Only Anrok and Dodo have an explicit non-resident column; Fonoa's 110+ table has no threshold column at all.
- No free table exposes a provenance layer (authority URL + date verified per row), so a dataset that does is differentiated on something visible rather than on country count.

### Gaps
- vatcalc.com's global tracker (likely the most complete independent list) could not be read because of bot protection; its country count, update cadence and any data-product pricing are unknown.
- I did not find any free public table that is both (a) specific to non-resident digital-services thresholds and (b) cited row-by-row to tax-authority documents.

## Key Question 3: Paid datasets/APIs for VAT thresholds and rates on digital services; prices; who buys them?

### Takeaway
Every rates API found (vatlayer, VATstack, vatcomply, vatnode, kdeldycke) is EU/Europe-only and rates-only; none returns registration thresholds or non-resident rules, and the global ones (Fonoa, Avalara, Stripe Tax API) are quote-based or transaction-priced tax engines rather than datasets.

### Cited Findings
- vatlayer (apilayer): Free $0 (100 requests/mo), Basic $9.99/mo ($107.99/yr, 1,000 requests, $0.04 overage), Professional $29.99/mo ($314.99/yr, 4,000, $0.03), Enterprise $99.99/mo ($1,019.99/yr, 20,000, $0.02); features: VAT number validation, EU VAT rates, price calculation; docs still reference "all 28 current EU member states"; no thresholds or digital-services fields — [vatlayer pricing](https://vatlayer.com/pricing); [vatlayer docs](https://docs.apilayer.com/vatlayer/docs/api-documentation)
- Vatstack: Developer free (dummy data); Launch "from $0/mo" (100 validations incl., $0.09 each extra; 20 "supplies" incl., $0.45 extra); Small Business from $15/mo (500 validations, $0.03; 100 supplies, $0.15); Enterprise from $150/mo (15,000 validations, $0.01; 3,000 supplies, $0.05; 99.95% SLA); VAT rates unlimited on all plans; "Rates for digital products" and "Evidence for place of supply" listed; coverage referenced as "EU (27)"; no registration-obligation logic — [Vatstack pricing](https://vatstack.com/pricing); [Vatstack rates](https://vatstack.com/rates)
- vatcomply: free API at api.vatcomply.com, no authentication, 2 requests/second per IP; EU-27 rates from the European Commission TEDB; open source (madisvain/vatcomply, MIT); no thresholds — [vatcomply](https://www.vatcomply.com/)
- vatnode eu-vat-rates-data: MIT; 45 European jurisdictions (EU-27 + 18 non-EU); EU rates pulled from the EC Taxes in Europe Database (TEDB) daily at 07:00 UTC, non-EU maintained manually from national sources; fields: standard/reduced/super-reduced/parking rates, VAT-ID formats; README explicitly says it does not include digital/e-services rates, registration thresholds or OSS provisions; 11 stars; language packages for JS/Python/PHP/Go/Ruby; PyPI version 2026.9.20 — [GitHub vatnode/eu-vat-rates-data](https://github.com/vatnode/eu-vat-rates-data); [PyPI eu-vat-rates-data](https://pypi.org/project/eu-vat-rates-data/2026.9.20/)
- kdeldycke/vat-rates: CSV database of digital/cloud/electronic services B2C VAT rates; 28 EU member states plus 57 special territories (some incomplete); fields start_date, stop_date, territory_codes, currency_code, rate, rate_type, description; BSD-2; 101 stars, 105 commits, 8 open issues; sources documented generally, not per row; README still refers to "28 EU member states" — [GitHub kdeldycke/vat-rates](https://github.com/kdeldycke/vat-rates)
- Other static EU JSON files: mikejolley/EU-VAT-Rates-for-Digital-Goods (JSON for WooCommerce), benbucksch/eu-vat-rates, ibericode/vat-rates, euvatrates.com — [GitHub mikejolley](https://github.com/mikejolley/EU-VAT-Rates-for-Digital-Goods); [GitHub benbucksch](https://github.com/benbucksch/eu-vat-rates); [GitHub ibericode](https://github.com/ibericode/vat-rates); [euvatrates.com](https://euvatrates.com/); GitHub topic listing — [tax-rates topic](https://github.com/topics/tax-rates?o=asc&s=stars)
- Fonoa tax-rate API exists ("Request a calculation") but pricing is custom/enterprise — [Fonoa docs](https://docs.fonoa.com/reference/requestataxrate-1); [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/fonoa-lookup)
- Stripe Tax API (standalone calculation) is priced the same 0.5% per transaction and its monitoring includes Tax API transactions by default — [Stripe Tax](https://stripe.com/tax); [Stripe monitoring docs](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/monitoring)
- Validation-API comparison from a vendor (Vatly) covers Vatly vs VATstack vs vatlayer vs VATsense — [Vatly guide](https://www.vatly.dev/guides/vat-validation-api-comparison)

### Inferences
- There is no paid or open dataset of non-resident digital-services registration thresholds with effective dates; the market has datasets for EU rates and VAT-ID validation, and tax engines that embed the rules opaquely. That is the whitespace.
- Buyers of the existing rate APIs are small SaaS/WooCommerce/Django sellers (plugins such as django-prices-vatlayer and the WooCommerce JSON file show the integration pattern) — [GitHub mirumee/django-prices-vatlayer](https://github.com/mirumee/django-prices-vatlayer).

### Gaps
- No public evidence of who licenses Fonoa/Avalara content as data (billing platforms, marketplaces) or at what price; vendor customer stories (e.g., Fonoa's "$45M ROI" communications-platform story) are not priced — [Fonoa customer story](https://www.fonoa.com/resources/customer-stories/global-comms-tax-invoicing-automation-45m-roi)
- vatnode.dev's commercial model (if any) behind the MIT dataset was not retrievable.

## Key Question 5: Known staleness/accuracy problems in public country tables that a provenance-first dataset could exploit

### Takeaway
Spot checks show the free tables lag 2025-2026 regime launches (Malawi, Botswana, Brazil, Sri Lanka absent from most) and, more damaging, mix domestic thresholds with non-resident thresholds (Avalara's Nigeria, Indonesia, Kenya, Vietnam rows) or contain unit errors (Quaderno's Nigeria "NGN 25,000"), while none dates or sources individual rows.

### Cited Findings

Recent regime changes (ground truth):
- Philippines: 12% VAT on digital services from June 2, 2025 (RA 12023; BIR RR 3-2025 and 14-2025); non-resident DSPs with annual gross sales above PHP 3 million must register via the VDS Portal; B2C VAT at checkout, B2B via withholding — [PwC PH](https://www.pwc.com/ph/en/tax/tax-publications/taxwise-or-otherwise/2025/soon-taking-effect-the-12-vat-on-digital-services.html); [Sovos](https://sovos.com/regulatory-updates/vat/philippines-imposes-12-vat-on-foreign-digital-service-providers/); [Taxumo on RR 3-2025](https://www.taxumo.com/blog/understanding-bir-revenue-regulations-3-2025-and-14-2025-vat-on-digital-services-in-the-philippines/); [Vertex](https://www.vertexinc.com/resources/resource-library/navigating-new-vat-rules-philippines-what-digital-businesses-need-know)
- Brazil: CBS/IBS reform; 2026 is a test year with symbolic rates (~0.9% CBS, 0.1% IBS); non-resident digital suppliers (SaaS, streaming, platforms, ads) must register for a CNPJ with Receita Federal, "no registration threshold and registration required since 1 August 2026"; platforms may be liable and financial institutions may withhold — [KPMG, May 2026](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/05/brazil-indirect-tax-reform-nonresident-sellers-digital-platforms.html); [vatcalc Brazil](https://www.vatcalc.com/brazil/brazil-vat-non-resident-digital-services-providers-marketplaces-2026/); [VATupdate Dec 2025](https://www.vatupdate.com/2025/12/02/brazils-tax-reform-new-cbs-ibs-rules-for-non-resident-digital-suppliers-from-2026/); [Global VAT Compliance](https://www.globalvatcompliance.com/globalvatnews/brazil-digital-vat-reform/)
- Malawi: VAT on non-resident digital services from 1 April 2026 at 17.5%; non-resident suppliers, intermediaries and marketplaces must register regardless of the MWK 50 million domestic threshold — [Fonoa Malawi](https://www.fonoa.com/resources/blog/malawi-vat-foreign-digital-services); [vatcalc Malawi](https://www.vatcalc.com/malawi/malawi-vat-on-non-digital-digital-services-april-2026/)
- Botswana: BURS introduces VAT obligations for non-resident digital service providers from 1 June 2026 at 14%; registration if taxable supplies exceed BWP 500,000 in 12 months — [vatcalc Botswana](https://www.vatcalc.com/botswana/botswana-vat-on-non-resident-digital-services/); [1stopVAT Botswana](https://1stopvat.com/botswana-vat-digital-services-non-resident-providers-2026/)
- South Africa: from 1 April 2025, foreign digital suppliers serving only VAT-registered SA businesses no longer need to register (reverse charge applies); intermediaries allowed — [vatcalc South Africa](https://www.vatcalc.com/south-africa/south-africa-vat-on-digital-services-by-non-residents/); [Polity](https://www.polity.org.za/article/new-vat-rules-for-digital-services-in-south-africa-what-businesses-need-to-know-2025-03-25)
- Nigeria: FIRS simplified compliance regime for non-resident suppliers (guidelines 11 Oct 2021; services from 1 Jan 2022) with a USD 25,000 per-12-months threshold; under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 obligations begin 1 January 2026 with a new portal and the same USD 25,000 threshold — [KPMG NG FIRS guidelines PDF](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ng/pdf/tax/guidelines-on-simplified-vat-compliance-regime-for-non-resident-suppliers.pdf); [BDO](https://www.bdo.global/en-gb/microsites/tax-newsletters/indirect-tax-news/issue-1-2022/nigeria-guidance-issued-on-simplified-vat-compliance-regime-for-nonresident-suppliers); [VATupdate Sept 2025](https://www.vatupdate.com/2025/09/11/nigeria-to-enforce-vat-on-non-resident-digital-services-from-2026/)
- Indonesia: foreign digital sellers are appointed PMSE VAT collectors above IDR 600 million/yr (or IDR 50 million/month) or 12,000 Indonesian users/yr (1,000/month); VAT 12% — [Commenda Indonesia](https://www.commenda.io/indonesia/vat-registration-for-foreign-companies); [1stopVAT Indonesia](https://1stopvat.com/indonesia-vat-digital-services-non-resident-providers); [ASEAN Briefing](https://www.aseanbriefing.com/news/indonesias-updated-vat-system-for-cross-border-digital-services/)
- Kenya: the KES 5 million threshold for non-resident digital suppliers was removed effective 1 July 2023; non-residents supplying via electronic network/digital marketplace must register regardless of turnover, VAT 16% — [KRA FAQ](https://www.kra.go.ke/helping-tax-payers/faqs/vat-on-digital-marketplace-supply); [vatcalc Kenya](https://www.vatcalc.com/kenya/kenya-vat-on-non-resident-digital-services-update-2/); [PwC WWTS Kenya](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/other-taxes)
- Sri Lanka: Stripe Tax lists Sri Lanka digital-products VAT "Supported since 7/1/2026", implying a 2026 non-resident regime — [Stripe supported countries](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/supported-countries)
- Forbes overview of 2025-2026 digital-services VAT changes — [Forbes, Mar 23 2025](https://www.forbes.com/sites/aleksandrabal/2025/03/23/global-vat-trends-new-tax-rules-for-digital-services-in-2025-and-2026/); "Digital services VAT rules tighten globally from 2026" — [Global VAT Compliance](https://www.globalvatcompliance.com/globalvatnews/digital-services-vat-2026/)

What the free tables show against that ground truth (fetched 2026-10-07):
- Avalara table: Philippines 12%/PHP 3 million (correct); Malawi, Botswana, Sri Lanka not listed; Brazil "VAT 21%", no threshold (Brazil has no single VAT; the CBS/IBS regime and Aug 2026 registration are not reflected); Nigeria "NGN 25 million" (domestic threshold, not the USD 25,000 non-resident threshold); Indonesia "IDR 4.8 billion" (domestic PKP threshold, not the IDR 600m/12,000-user PMSE rule) and 11% (rate is 12%); Kenya "KES 5 million" (removed July 2023); Vietnam "VND 100 million"; no last-updated date; no sources — [Avalara table](https://www.avalara.com/vatlive/en/global-vat-gst-on-e-services.html)
- Quaderno table (updated Apr 15, 2026): Philippines 12%/PHP 3,000,000 (correct, no effective date); Malawi, Botswana, Brazil, Peru, Sri Lanka, Pakistan not listed; Nigeria "7.5% VAT with NGN 25,000 threshold" (unit/currency error vs FIRS USD 25,000); Indonesia 11% with IDR 600,000,000 (threshold correct, rate stale); Kenya zero threshold (correct); Vietnam zero threshold — [Quaderno table](https://quaderno.io/blog/digital-taxes-around-world-know-new-tax-rules/)
- Fonoa (updated Mar 4, 2026): Philippines present at 12%; Brazil and Malawi absent; Botswana mentioned only as "planning"; no threshold column — [Fonoa](https://www.fonoa.com/resources/blog/global-vat-and-gst-on-digital-services)
- Dodo (Sept 20, 2026): Philippines PHP 3m present; Brazil present with reform note; Malawi and Botswana absent — [Dodo](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/vat-gst-registration-thresholds)
- VATupdate/vatabout (Oct 1, 2026): no mention of Philippines, Brazil, Malawi, Botswana changes; Indonesia shown as "12% (11% effective)" — [VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/10/01/vat-on-digital-services-by-country-rates-thresholds-and-registration-rules/)
- Stripe Tax: Brazil, Malawi, Botswana, Pakistan absent from supported locations, so no monitoring there — [Stripe supported countries](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/supported-countries)
- Rate churn: 27 jurisdictions changed VAT rates in 2025 (OECD via VATupdate/vatcalc) — [VATupdate on OECD](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/09/21/oecd-report-highlights-global-shift-to-digital-vat-enforcement/)

### Inferences
- The highest-value correction a provenance-first dataset can make is structural, not just freshness: separating "domestic registration threshold" from "non-resident digital-services threshold" (Avalara conflates them in at least four rows) and attaching the legal instrument and effective date per row (Kenya's removed KES 5m threshold is a three-year-old change still shown).
- Second-order fields that no free table carries and that decide registration for SaaS sellers: B2B treatment (reverse charge vs withholding, e.g., Philippines withholding, South Africa 2025 reverse charge), platform/marketplace deemed-supplier rules (Brazil, Malawi), and user-count triggers (Indonesia 12,000 users).

### Gaps
- Vietnam's non-resident threshold (Avalara VND 100m vs Quaderno zero) was not independently verified here.
- Avalara's Brazil "21%" figure could not be traced to any source.
- I did not re-verify every row; the discrepancies above come from targeted spot checks of 12 countries.

## Key Question 6: How big is the audience (non-US SaaS sellers into EU/UK/AU) and what do they search for?

### Takeaway
The only hard official number is the EU non-Union OSS scheme: 5,628 registered non-EU traders at end-2024 (+6% YoY) declaring €2.8bn of VAT, versus 153,550 Union-scheme traders; aggregator estimates put ~42,000 SaaS companies worldwide with roughly 70% outside the US, and the search landscape is dense with vendor-written "EU VAT for SaaS / indie hackers 2026" guides, but no keyword-volume data was found.

### Cited Findings
- Non-Union OSS registrations: 5,628 traders at 31 Dec 2024 vs 5,313 in 2023 (+315, +6%); Union OSS 153,550 vs 132,609 (+20,941, +16%); IOSS 12,799 vs 12,087; IOSS intermediaries 1,349; "Over 170,000 traders were registered" in total; report dated July 14, 2025 by DG TAXUD — [EU Commission: Report on the application of the VAT e-commerce package for 2024](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/news-reports/report-application-vat-e-commerce-package-2024_en)
- VAT declared 2024: Union OSS over €24bn; non-Union OSS €2.8bn; IOSS €6.3bn; total €33.1bn, +26% vs €26.3bn in 2023; nearly €88bn cumulative since 2021 — [EU Commission news, 23 July 2025](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/continued-growth-revenue-and-registrations-confirms-success-reformed-eu-vat-rules-e-commerce-2025-07-23_en); [vatcalc summary](https://www.vatcalc.com/eu/eu-oss-and-ioss-delivers-over-e26bn-vat-revenues/)
- OECD: >116 jurisdictions apply OECD-style digital-trade VAT rules, 22 more implementing/considering (so a non-US seller faces up to ~116 potential registration decisions) — [OECD press release](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/news/press-releases/2026/09/governments-reforming-tax-systems-to-boost-growth-but-revenue-pressures-continue-to-mount.html)

### Inferences
- The gap between 5,628 non-Union OSS registrants and tens of thousands of non-EU software sellers (even taking the aggregator counts loosely) indicates most non-EU digital sellers are either using an MoR, under-registered, or not yet selling B2C into the EU; a free wizard's realistic audience is the long tail that Stripe ignores (<$10k) and that cannot justify $24-35/month tooling.
- The query space ("do I need to register for EU VAT", "OSS for SaaS", "sell digital products in Europe 2026") is already crowded with vendor content; differentiation would need to come from the provenance/accuracy angle and from non-EU coverage (Philippines, Nigeria, Indonesia, Brazil, Malawi, Botswana), where the incumbent tables are weakest.

### Gaps
- No keyword-volume data (Google Trends/Ahrefs) was obtainable; search intent is inferred from competing page titles only.
- No official counts of non-resident registrations for the UK (HMRC), Australia (ATO simplified GST), Canada, Japan or Singapore were found; the ATO pages fetched contain requirements but no registrant statistics.
- SaaS company counts come from marketing-statistics aggregators with undisclosed methodology; no primary source (e.g., Crunchbase/Dealroom export) was retrieved.
